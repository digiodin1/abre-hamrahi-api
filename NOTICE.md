The bundled IRANSansX font files are third-party assets supplied for this project.
They are not covered by this project's MIT software license. Font use and
redistribution are governed by the font vendor's license. The Android TTF files
are derived from the corresponding bundled WOFF2 files with FontTools.

Build tools, test dependencies and their licenses remain with their respective
distributions. Android signing keys, private profiles and account data are never
included in the repository or release bundles.
